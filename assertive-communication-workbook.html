POWER SKILL #2
WHY ASSERTIVE COMMUNICATION IS HARD

Sometimes you know what you want to say and the words just don't come. In the moment, going along feels safer and easier than the friction of speaking up, so you stay quiet and feel it later. Speaking up takes practice, not a personality change.

This workbook uses assertive communication worksheets to hand you fill-in-the-blank scripts you can borrow until they feel like your own.

What's Inside

Nine levels to work through

It starts with a quick quiz, “Secret Identity Check-In”, to find your starting point. Then you level up through hero-style missions, one small win at a time.

Level 1

Secret Identity Check-In

Spot whether you tend to go passive, aggressive, or assertive, so you know your starting point.

Level 2

The 3-Part Assertive Message

Learn a simple behavior, feeling, effect formula for saying the hard thing without blaming.

Level 3

Assertive Sentence Starters

Collect a few go-to opening lines, so you don’t have to find the words from scratch.

Level 4

Mirror Practice

Rehearse your lines out loud, so they feel natural when the real moment comes.

Level 5

Planning for Pushback

Plan your next move for when someone pushes back, so one reaction doesn’t throw you off.

Level 6

The Sandwich Technique

Wrap tough feedback between two honest positives, so it’s easier for the other person to hear.

Level 7

Speak Up Tracker

Log the times you spoke up and how it went, so you can see what’s getting easier.

Level 8

Reflect

Look back at what’s changed and what you’re proud of, and notice how far you’ve come.

Level 9

You’re on Your Way

Choose your next move and one small step to keep practicing, in real life or your next Power Skill.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is the workbook really free?

Yes. The 38-page Adulting Like A Champ workbook is free. Enter your email and we will send it over. No payment, and no trial that turns into a charge.

Who is it for?

It was built for neurodivergent teens and young adults stepping into independence, plus the parents, teachers, and coaches who support them. That said, the skills inside are useful for just about anybody learning to run their own life.

Do I print it or fill it on a screen?

Either works. It is a fillable PDF, so you can type into it on a device or print it and use a pen. No app required.

Is this a clinical or diagnostic tool?

No. Adulting Like A Champ is a coaching and learning tool. It does not assess, diagnose, or treat anything. It helps you build everyday life skills, one mission at a time.

What happens after I sign up?

We will email your workbook and set up a free library where it lives, so it is easy to find later. You can unsubscribe from emails anytime and still keep your workbook.

How is this different from the Real-Life Executive Functioning Workbook?

Adulting Like A Champ is a whole-person guide to the skills adulthood asks for. The Real-Life Executive Functioning Workbook is a deeper dive into executive function specifically. Different tools, same shelf.
