Amy Kim Waschke

Client Services Coordinator

Amy Kim Waschke has been in her role at Life Skills Advocate (LSA) for several years. She deeply enjoys supporting the neurodivergent community and has repeatedly witnessed the transformative change LSA's clients achieve through the coaching process.

Amy's primary focus involves communication with clients, coaches, and community organizations. She brings a diverse professional background to her role, including experience in arts education, personal assisting, and property management. Additionally, she is certified in Everything DiSC, an assessment model that enhances communication and understanding across different personality types, and has worked as a public speaking coach. She holds both a BA and an MFA from the University of Washington.

Outside of LSA, Amy is a passionate theater maker and is a longtime acting company member at the Oregon Shakespeare Festival.
