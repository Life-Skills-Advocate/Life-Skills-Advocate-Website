POWER SKILL #5
WHY CONFLICT MANAGEMENT IS HARD

Conflict tends to go one of two ways: you shut down, or it boils over. That’s your body’s alarm going off, the stress response fires before the thinking part of your brain catches up, so you react before you can choose how to. Neither way leaves you feeling heard.

There’s a middle path, and it’s learnable. This workbook gives you a pause-and-plan routine, with conflict resolution worksheets, so a hard conversation doesn’t get to run the show.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Eight levels to work through

It starts with a quick quiz, “Know Your Conflict Style”, to find your starting point. Then you level up through hero-style missions, one small win at a time.

Level 1

Know Your Conflict Style

Take a quick, no-wrong-answers quiz to see how you tend to handle conflict, so you know your starting point.

Level 2

Freeze or Fume Meter

Learn to read your body’s signals and tell whether you’re sliding toward shutting down or blowing up.

Level 3

Pause Button

Build a pause you can actually use, so you can settle your body before you say anything.

Level 4

Cool It Down Toolbox

Collect a handful of calm-down and resolution strategies, then keep the few that work for you.

Level 5

Plan Your Words, Not Your Win

Plan a few clear sentences ahead of time, so you can say what you mean without it turning into a fight.

Level 6

Talk It Out

Map a real conflict as a short comic strip, so you can slow it down and picture a better ending.

Level 7

Reflect

Look back at what’s gotten easier and what you’re proud of, and notice how far you’ve come.

Level 8

You’re on Your Way

Choose your next move and one small step to keep practicing, in real situations or your next Power Skill.
