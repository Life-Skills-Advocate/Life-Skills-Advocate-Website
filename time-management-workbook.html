POWER SKILL #6
WHY TIME MANAGEMENT IS HARD

An hour feels like ten minutes, the day disappears, and you’re not sure where it went. Time is invisible: there’s nothing to see or feel passing, so a brain that’s deep in something can lose track of it completely. That’s a real wiring difference, not carelessness.

Good time management starts with making time visible, seeing where it goes, then adding a few tools that fit how your brain works. This workbook of time management worksheets builds both.
