POWER SKILL #7
WHY PLANNING & PRIORITIZING IS HARD

When everything lands at once and feels equally urgent, there’s no clear signal for what comes first. So instead of picking, your brain stalls, and you freeze with the whole pile in front of you. That’s overwhelm, not laziness.

Knowing how to prioritize tasks is a skill you can build, not a knack you’re born with. This workbook breaks the big, scary pile into one small next move, then the one after that.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Eight levels to work through

It starts with a quick quiz, “Chaos vs. Control”, to find your starting point. Then you level up through hero-style missions, one small win at a time.

Level 1

Chaos vs. Control Quiz

A quick quiz that shows whether you lean toward chaos, control, or somewhere in between, so you know your starting point.

Level 2

Three-Step Prioritizing Path

Brain-dump everything, sort it by what really matters, and pick your top three for the day.

Level 3

Break Down the Beast

Turn one big, scary task into the smallest possible steps, then start with just the first.

Level 4

Protecting Your Planning Time

Build a short, repeatable planning ritual and put it somewhere it won’t get pushed aside.

Level 5

Calendars, Checklists & Color Codes

Try out planning tools and keep the one or two that actually fit your brain.

Level 6

SMART Goal: Plan Smarter, Not Harder

Turn ‘be more organized’ into one small, specific goal you can practice over two weeks.

Level 7

Planning in Action

Look at a time a plan fell apart and build a smarter adjustment for next time.

Level 8

You’re on Your Way

Look at how far you’ve come and choose your next small step.
