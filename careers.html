About Life Skills Advocate

Meet Our Team Discover the LSA Difference Our Coaching Process Our Core Values FAQs the EF Ripple Effect Explained

Serving the greater Seattle region, Life Skills Advocate is an executive functioning and life skills coaching and transition support company that empowers neurodiverse teens, adults and families to become their own best advocates and teaches them how to maximize their autonomy and independence.

We provide in-home and virtual support across daily living, academic, vocational and community-based domains.

Our mission: Uplifting the neurodivergent community to embrace their strengths and self-advocate with confidence.

Meet The Founder

About Chris.

Life Skills Advocate was founded in 2019 by Chris Hanson, who has personal experience with ADHD, anxiety & depression and understands the importance of having strong advocates on your side. Chris started Life Skills Advocate because he wanted to create the type of support service he wishes he had when he was a teenager struggling to find his path in life.

Professionally, Chris holds a Bachelor of Science in Special Education and spent 8+ years as a high school special education teacher and paraeducator in the Kent School District. Over time, he saw a critical need to support students (and adults) in becoming their own best advocates, and helping them build the confidence and skills needed to tackle what's getting in the way of pursuing their goals and having a higher high quality of life.
