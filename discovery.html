Frequently Asked Questions

What is executive function coaching?

Executive function coaching helps individuals build skills and systems for planning, organizing, time management, task initiation, emotional regulation, and follow-through so they can manage real-life demands with less stress.

Why choose Life Skills Advocate?

We pair lived experience with professional expertise. Our coaches understand neurodivergent strengths and challenges and support clients in a shame-free, collaborative, practical way.

How does the coaching process work?

We begin with a complimentary discovery meeting, identify your goals and needs, and then match you with the right coach for a supportive, personalized coaching plan.

What are the backgrounds of your coaches?

Our coaches bring a blend of lived neurodivergent experience, professional training, and real-world experience supporting students, young adults, and adults in building useful skills.
