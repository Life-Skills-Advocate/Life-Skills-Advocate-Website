POWER SKILL #1
WHY ASKING FOR HELP IS HARD

Asking for help can feel risky. It means risking a no and admitting out loud that you're stuck, so your brain can read a simple ask as exposure and steer you away from it. You push through alone instead. That's common, and it isn't a character flaw.

Asking is a skill you can build. This workbook uses asking for help worksheets to make the ask smaller, clearer, and easier to say out loud.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Nine levels to work through

It starts with a quick quiz, “Ask-For-Help Checklist”, to find your starting point. Then you level up through hero-style missions, one small win at a time.

Level 1

Ask-For-Help Checklist

Spot the early signs that it’s time to ask, so you call for backup before you’re completely stuck.

Level 2

Barriers to Asking

Name the thoughts that stop you from asking, then swap each one for a kinder, truer thought.

Level 3

Build Your Help Network

Map out who you can go to and what they’re good at, so you’re not scrambling in the moment.

Level 4

Script It Out

Write out a short, clear ask ahead of time, so the words are ready when you need them.

Level 5

Body Language Booster

Pick a few small body-language tweaks that help your ask land the way you mean it.

Level 6

Face the “No” and Keep Going

Plan for a yes, a no, or a maybe, so one answer doesn’t stop you in your tracks.

Level 7

A Hero’s Story

Tell a short story of a time asking for help paid off, so reaching out starts to feel normal.

Level 8

Reflect

Look back at what’s gotten easier and what you’re proud of, and notice how far you’ve come.

Level 9

You’re on Your Way

Choose your next move and one small step to keep practicing, in real life or your next Power Skill.
