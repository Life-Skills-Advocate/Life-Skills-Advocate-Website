POWER SKILL #3
WHY ACTIVE LISTENING IS HARD

Listening looks easy, but it's a lot at once: holding your own thoughts, reading tone and body language, and resisting the urge to jump in. When your mind moves fast, that's genuinely hard, and it can leave you a step behind or a little misunderstood.

That's not a character flaw, and it's not permanent. Listening is a skill you can build. This workbook uses active listening worksheets to turn it into small, low-pressure practice rounds, so conversations start to feel more connected.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Nine levels to work through

It starts with a quick quiz, “Secret Identity Check-In”, to find your starting point. Then you level up through hero-style missions, one small win at a time.

Level 1

Secret Identity Check-In

Spot your usual listening style, so you know where you’re starting from.

Level 2

The Watch and Listen Game

Practice reading tone, body language, and words, so you catch more of what people mean.

Level 3

The Filter

Practice waiting before you jump in, so you stop finishing other people’s sentences.

Level 4

Reflective Listening Practice

Reflect back what you heard, so the other person feels understood.

Level 5

Non-Verbal Listening Cues

Use eye contact, nodding, and posture, so your body shows you’re tuned in.

Level 6

The 10-Minute Listen

Build your focus stamina, so you can stay with longer conversations.

Level 7

High-Stakes Listening

Stay calm and tuned in during conflict, so a tough talk doesn’t throw you off.

Level 8

Reflect

Look back at what’s changed and what you’re proud of, and notice how far you’ve come.

Level 9

You’re on Your Way

Choose your next move and one small step to keep practicing, in real life or your next Power Skill.
