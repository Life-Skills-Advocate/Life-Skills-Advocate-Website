POWER SKILL #4
WHY SETTING BOUNDARIES IS HARD

When “no” feels impossible, you say yes to everything and quietly resent it later. In the moment, the discomfort of disappointing someone feels bigger than the cost of taking it all on, so “yes” slips out before you’ve decided. The cost lands later, in time and energy you didn’t have.

Boundaries aren’t about being difficult. They’re how you protect your time and energy, and they get easier with practice. This workbook of boundaries worksheets lets you rehearse the “no” until it’s yours, in your own words.
