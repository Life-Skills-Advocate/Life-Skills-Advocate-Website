Executive Function Coaching Pricing

At Life Skills Advocate, we want clients and families to understand the investment before scheduling a discovery meeting. Coaching is a meaningful investment, and we believe clear pricing helps everyone make a more informed decision about fit.

Take The First Step

$220

per 50-minute coaching session

What to Expect When Starting Coaching

We recommend that new clients plan for a minimum of 12 weekly 50-minute sessions when beginning services.

Foundation Session

$220

A 50-minute initial session focused on getting to know you or your loved one, discussing goals, and laying the framework.

Weekly Coaching

$220/session

Through weekly 50-minute sessions, one-on-one with your coach, you'll build rapport, identify strengths and challenges, and practice strategies in real-time.

Recommended Starting Investment

$2,640

This is the total investment for twelve 50-minute sessions one-on-one with an executive function coach.

Why We Recommend Starting With 12 Sessions

Meaningful change takes time. While every client’s path is different, 12 sessions gives the coach and client enough time to build trust, identify a meaningful goal, practice strategies, and begin to see progress. This does not mean every client will finish coaching after 12 sessions. Some clients continue longer depending on their goals, support needs, and progress.

  • Build rapport with the coach
  • Clarify goals and support needs
  • Identify executive functioning patterns
  • Practice strategies between sessions
  • Adjust tools that are not working
  • Begin building confidence and independence

What's Included in Coaching

1:1 Executive Function Coaching

Personalized coaching focused on the client’s goals, challenges, and daily life.

Goal-Setting and Strategy Development

Support with identifying meaningful goals and building practical tools to work toward them.

Weekly Accountability

Consistent support to help clients practice strategies and reflect on what is or is not working.

Neurodivergent-Affirming Support

A coaching approach that respects each client’s nervous system, needs, strengths, and lived experience.

Practical Tools for Daily Life

Strategies that can be applied to school, work, home, routines, relationships, and independence.

Appropriate Collaboration

When relevant and consented to, coaching may include communication with family members or support people.

Why Our Rate Is What It Is

We know coaching is an investment. Our rate reflects individualized 1:1 support, coach training and supervision, preparation and documentation, and our commitment to compensating our team sustainably.

We keep our pricing straightforward so clients and families can understand the investment before moving forward.

Is Coaching a Good Fit Financially Right Now?

Coaching may be a good fit right now if:

  • You are prepared to invest in weekly support
  • You are ready for the effort both inside and outside of sessions to see meaningful change
  • You are looking for practical skill-building, strategies, and gentle accountability
  • You understand that coaching is private-pay
  • You are ready to work on one meaningful goal or challenge area over time

Coaching may not be the right fit right now if:

  • The cost would create significant financial strain
  • You need a service that bills health insurance directly
  • You are primarily needing mental health support, crisis support, or case management
  • You are needing in-person support
  • You are not able to commit to consistent sessions at this time
