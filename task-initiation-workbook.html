POWER SKILL #8
WHY TASK INITIATION IS HARD

You know what to do, you want to do it, and you still can’t start. Starting a task runs on a different mental gear than doing it, and for a lot of neurodivergent brains that gear is the one that sticks. So you can fully intend to begin and still sit there at the starting line.

That gap has a name, task initiation, and it isn’t laziness or weak willpower. It’s a skill you can build. This workbook shrinks the first step down to something small enough to take, then the next one.
