Neurodivergent-friendly Tools & Resources

Discover carefully selected tools and resources for neurodivergent individuals, their families, and those who support them.

Prioritizing quality over quantity, our curated list includes ND-friendly products, books, housing, mental health providers, learning platforms, software, professional organizers, and more. With a focus on both global and local Seattle-based resources, our team at LSA is committed to recommending valuable, easy-to-understand tools that truly empower the ND community.

Important: Please Read Before Proceeding

At Life Skills Advocate (LSA), we're committed to helping neurodivergent individuals and families achieve autonomy and independence. Before recommending anything, our team carefully reviews each tool and resource, ensuring they're suitable for our diverse audience.

We sometimes partner with companies as affiliates, providing valuable tools we believe will genuinely benefit you. When you click a link for a tool and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Our recommendations stem from our deep experience and knowledge of these companies and products. We never suggest a product just for commission, as doing so would not align with our core values. We encourage you to invest in tools only if they align with your goals and values.

That being said, this page is fluid. We will update and expand this page periodically to provide the most current and valuable resources we know about.
